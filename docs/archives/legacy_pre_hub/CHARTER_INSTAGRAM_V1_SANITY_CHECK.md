# Charter vs Instagram v1 — Sanity Check

**Purpose:** Constraint check to ensure Instagram v1 is neither blocked nor too loose relative to the Image Capability Charter.

**Authoritative policy:** `docs/IMAGE_CAPABILITY_CHARTER.md`

---

## Does the Charter over-constrain Instagram?

**No.**

**Reasoning:**

- 3-image carousels are fully compatible:
  - Slide 1: `typographic_card`
  - Slide 2: `public_domain_historical` or `generated_editorial_illustration`
  - Slide 3: reinforcement (any allowed class)
- Generated images are allowed as long as:
  - They are illustrative
  - They avoid named people/events
  - They carry disclosure (caption/footer/meta)

Nothing in the Charter forces:

- Photorealism
- Expensive sourcing
- Heavy attribution overlays

---

## Does the Charter under-constrain Instagram?

**No** — it closes the risky gaps.

It explicitly prevents:

- “Fake historical scenes” in carousels
- AI images that look like documentary photos
- Silent use of generated images without disclosure
- Coders inventing new image types ad hoc

These are exactly the failure modes Instagram encourages if left vague.

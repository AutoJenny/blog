Blog Posts

Blog Posts are longer-form articles published on the blog website. These are different from social media posts - they appear on the main blog and can be shared to social media.

Blog Post Types

  • Themed Posts - Posts organized around specific themes or topics
  • Recipe Posts - Recipe and cooking content
  • Profile Posts - Profiles of people, businesses, or organizations
  • Generated Posts - LLM-generated blog articles created automatically from product or category data

What Are Generated Posts?

Generated Posts are blog articles (not social media posts) that are automatically created using LLM technology. The system:

  • Takes product or category data as source material
  • Uses LLM (Ollama) to generate a full blog article with title, standfirst, and multiple sections
  • Creates a draft blog post that can be reviewed and edited before publication
  • Once published, these appear on the blog website like any other blog post

Note: Generated Posts create blog articles, not social media posts. For social media posts about products, see Product Posts.

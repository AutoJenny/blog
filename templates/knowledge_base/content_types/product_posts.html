Product Posts

{{ get_status_badge('in_progress')|safe }}

Product Posts are social media posts that feature products from the Clan.com catalog. These are short-form social media posts (not blog articles) designed for platforms like Facebook and Instagram. The system includes a UI for product selection, AI-powered content generation, and posting queue management.

What Are Product Posts?

Product Posts are social media content that:

  • Feature Products: Showcase products from the Clan.com catalog
  • Social Media Format: Short captions, product images, hashtags
  • AI Content Generation: Use Ollama LLM to generate engaging product descriptions
  • Direct Posting: Posted directly to social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
  • Queue Management: Schedule and manage multiple posts in a queue

System Components

1. Item Selection

The system allows you to:

  • Browse products by category from the Clan.com catalog
  • View product details (name, SKU, price, description, images)
  • Search and filter products
  • Select a product for content generation

2. AI Content Generation

Uses Ollama LLM to generate social media captions:

  • Content Types: Feature-focused, benefit-focused, or story-focused posts
  • Template System: Configurable prompt templates for different content styles
  • Ollama Integration: Automatic AI service management (starts if not running)
  • Content Preview: Real-time preview of generated content
  • Regeneration: Generate new content variations

3. Posting Queue

Manage scheduled posts:

  • Timeline View: See all scheduled posts chronologically
  • Drag-and-Drop: Reorder posts by dragging
  • Status Tracking: Draft, Ready, Published, Failed
  • Batch Operations: Generate multiple posts at once
  • Time Slot Generation: Automatic scheduling based on patterns

4. Posting Control

Control when and how posts are published:

  • Schedule posts for specific dates and times
  • Post immediately or schedule for later
  • Manage posting schedules (recurring patterns)

How It Works

  1. Select Product: Browse and select a product from the catalog
  2. Generate Content: Use AI to generate an engaging social media caption
  3. Add to Queue: Add the generated post to the posting queue
  4. Schedule: Set date and time for posting (or post immediately)
  5. Publish: System posts to Facebook at scheduled time (or manually trigger)

UI Access

Product Posts can be managed through:

  • Product Posts Interface - Main interface for creating and managing product posts
  • Monitoring Dashboard - View posting status and automation events
  • Publication Dashboard - Overall publication status (product posts may not appear here yet)

Database Storage

Product posts are stored in the posting_queue table:

  • content_type = 'product' - Identifies as product post
  • product_id - Links to product in catalog
  • generated_content - JSON with caption and metadata
  • scheduled_date and scheduled_time - When to post
  • status - Draft, Ready, Published, Failed
  • platform - Facebook, Instagram, etc.

Difference from Generated Posts

Product Posts (this page) are social media posts about products - short posts for Facebook/Instagram.

Generated Posts (under Blog Posts) are blog articles generated from products - full-length articles for the blog website.

Both use products as source material, but create different types of content for different channels.

Configuration

Product posts use:

  • Database: posting_queue table for storage
  • AI Service: Ollama (local LLM) for content generation
  • Product Data: Clan.com catalog integration
  • Facebook API: Graph API for posting (both pages)
  • Scheduling: daily_posts_schedule table for recurring patterns

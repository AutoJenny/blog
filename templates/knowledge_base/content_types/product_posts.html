Product Posts

{{ get_status_badge('complete')|safe }}

Product Posts are social media posts that feature products from the Clan.com catalog. These are short-form social media posts (not blog articles) designed for platforms like Facebook and Instagram. The system is fully automated and uses the same workflow system as weekly content.

✅ Fully Automated: Product posts are now fully automated with workflow integration, prompt standardization, and automated scripts. See Product Posts All Phases Complete for complete documentation.

What Are Product Posts?

Product Posts are social media content that:

  • Feature Products: Showcase products from the Clan.com catalog
  • Social Media Format: Short captions, product images, hashtags
  • AI Content Generation: Use Ollama LLM to generate engaging product descriptions
  • Direct Posting: Posted directly to social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
  • Queue Management: Schedule and manage multiple posts in a queue

How It Works (Fully Automated)

Product posts are now fully automated:

  1. Automatic Creation: automated_product_post_creator.py creates draft posts 1 week ahead based on schedules
  2. Automatic Workflow: automated_product_post_workflow.py executes workflow stages:
    • Formats product data
    • Generates caption (30 style variations)
    • Adds hashtags
    • Uses product image URL
  3. Automatic Scheduling: automated_posting.py schedules posts with exact timestamps
  4. Automatic Publishing: posting_executor.py posts to Facebook at scheduled time

See Product Post Automation for complete details.

UI Access

Database Storage

Product posts are stored in the posting_queue table:

  • content_type = 'product' - Identifies as product post
  • product_id - Links to product in catalog
  • generated_content - JSON with caption and metadata
  • scheduled_date and scheduled_time - When to post
  • status - Draft, Ready, Published, Failed
  • platform - Facebook, Instagram, etc.

Difference from Generated Posts

Product Posts (this page) are social media posts about products - short posts for Facebook/Instagram.

Generated Posts (under Blog Posts) are blog articles generated from products - full-length articles for the blog website.

Both use products as source material, but create different types of content for different channels.

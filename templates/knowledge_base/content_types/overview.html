Content Types Overview

Content types are organized into two main categories: Blog Posts (for the blog website) and Social Posts (for social media platforms).

Blog Posts

Longer-form articles published on the blog website:

  • Themed Posts - Posts organized around specific themes
  • Recipe Posts - Recipe and cooking content
  • Profile Posts - Profiles of people, businesses, or organizations
  • Generated Posts - 🟡 In Progress - LLM-generated blog articles from products/categories

Social Posts

Short-form content for social media platforms:

  • Weekly Words & Phrases - ✅ Complete - Automated Scots language content
  • Product Posts - ✅ Complete - Automated social media posts featuring products

Key Distinction

Blog Posts = Full-length articles for the blog website

Social Posts = Short posts for social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)

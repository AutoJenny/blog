Social Posts

Social Posts are content created specifically for social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.). These are shorter-form posts designed for social engagement, different from longer blog articles.

Social Post Types

  • Weekly Words & Phrases - ✅ Complete - Automated Scots language content (words, phrases, insults) posted to Facebook
  • Product Posts - 🔴 Planned - Social media posts featuring products from the catalog

Key Differences from Blog Posts

  • Length: Social posts are shorter, optimized for quick engagement
  • Format: Designed for social media platforms (square images, captions, hashtags)
  • Purpose: Direct social engagement rather than in-depth articles
  • Distribution: Posted directly to social platforms, not to the blog website

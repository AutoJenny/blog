Weekly Words & Phrases

{{ get_status_badge('complete')|safe }}

Weekly Words & Phrases is a fully automated system for creating and publishing weekly Scots language content (words, phrases, and insults) to Facebook. This system is complete and in production.

Content Types

Three types of weekly language content are supported:

  • Weekly Words - Single Scots words with translation, usage examples, and provenance
  • Weekly Phrases - Scots phrases with translation and provenance
  • Weekly Insults - Scots insults with translation and provenance

How It Works

1. Content Creation

Content is stored in the calendar_ideas table and scheduled via the calendar system.

  • Words include: Scots text, translation, two usage examples, and provenance
  • Phrases and insults include: Scots text, translation, and provenance

2. Automated Workflow

The system automatically:

  1. Creates posts - 1 week in advance based on calendar schedule
  2. Formats content - Extracts and formats data for social media
  3. Generates captions - Uses Ollama with 30 style variations
  4. Adds hashtags - Adds appropriate hashtags
  5. Generates images - Creates 1080×1080 square images with ImageMagick
  6. Publishes - Posts to both Facebook pages at scheduled time

3. Image Generation

Images are generated with:

  • 96pt italic Baskerville font for main Scots text
  • Automatic text wrapping for long phrases/insults
  • Usage examples in cream italics (words only)
  • Provenance in grey text
  • Branded logo in corner

Output Channels

Output channels:

  • Facebook - Posts to both pages
  • Instagram - 🔴 Planned
  • Twitter - 🔴 Planned

UI Access

Weekly content can be managed through:

  • Calendar View - View and manage calendar schedule
  • Monitoring Dashboard - Monitor automation status

Configuration

Weekly content uses the following configuration:

  • Workflow: config/output_channel_stages.py - Defines stages for weekly content → Facebook
  • Image Styling: config/weekly_content_image_config.py - Colors, fonts, layout
  • Caption Prompts: config/weekly_content_caption_prompts.py - 30 style variations

System Overview

The blog application is a comprehensive content management and publication system for creating and publishing various types of content across multiple channels.

Core Concepts

Content Types (Post Types)

The system supports multiple content types, each with its own workflow:

  • Themed Posts - Regular blog posts with full planning and research workflow
  • Recipe Posts - Recipe content with specialized workflow
  • Profile Posts - Product or surname profile content
  • Weekly Content - Automated weekly word/phrase/insult posts (Word, Phrase, Insult)
  • Generated Posts - AI-generated content posts

Output Channels

Content can be published to multiple channels:

  • Blog - Main website blog
  • Facebook - Social media posts (fully automated for weekly content)
  • Instagram - Image-based social media (in development)
  • Twitter - Short-form social media (planned)
  • Newsletter - Email newsletter system

Workflow Stages

Content goes through defined stages:

  • Calendar - Scheduling and week planning
  • Planning - Content planning and structure
  • Research - Research and source gathering (themed posts)
  • Authoring - Content creation and drafting
  • Imaging - Image generation and optimization
  • Header - Title, summary, SEO, and final review

Key Systems

Automation

The system includes extensive automation:

  • Weekly Content Automation - Fully automated creation, processing, and publishing
  • Product Post Automation - Automated product post scheduling
  • Background Monitor - Orchestrates all automated processes

Management Interfaces

Various interfaces for managing content:

  • Planning Interface - Calendar, ideas, taxonomy, structure
  • Authoring Interface - Drafting, image concepts, prompts, captions
  • Imaging Interface - Image generation and optimization
  • Header Interface - Title, summary, SEO, final review
  • Publication Dashboard - Overview of publication status
  • Monitoring System - Real-time automation monitoring
  • Launchpad - One-click publication and syndication tools
